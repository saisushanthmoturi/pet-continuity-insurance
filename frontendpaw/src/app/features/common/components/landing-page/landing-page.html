How Continuity Protection Operates

A seamless blend of legal trust instruments, digital allowance distribution, and ongoing caretaker verification.

1

Dedicated Care Trust

We establish an actuarially calculated escrow care fund specifically earmarked for your pet's future needs, protected from third-party probate disputes.

2

Designate Guardians

Assign trusted friends, family, or partners as Primary and Backup caretakers. If unavailable, our vetted licensed caregiver sanctuary network steps in.

3

Automated Stipends

Upon claim verification, automated monthly care allowances are disbursed to the guardian for nutritious food, grooming, and standard daily essentials.

4

Direct Vet Escrow

Veterinary emergencies and regular treatments are settled directly with licensed animal clinics via our dedicated medical reserve account.
